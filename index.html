ⓘ How It Works ▼
1
Pick a layout below — center, inline, compact, framed, minimal or pyramid. Each one arranges the lines differently.
2
Edit each line. A gift has six fields: Deco top, Top line, Main text, Bottom line, Kaomoji and Deco bottom. Type your own text or click a chip suggestion.
3
Style each field with the little color square next to the input — pick a color, a gradient, or toggle "no color" to save characters. Font toggle (Aa / αв / ꜱᴄ) switches normal, fancy or small caps.
4
Reorder or remove lines directly in the preview — hover any line and use the ▲ / ▼ arrows to move it up or down, or the ✕ button to clear it. Works in Center, Framed, Minimal and Pyramid layouts. The generated code below updates instantly.
5
Browse the templates on the right — Sweet, Funny, Flirty, Dominant, Spicy, Roast, Birthday and more. One click fills all three text lines at once.
6
Watch the counter in the topbar. 3dxchat allows 240 chars / 255 bytes. If it turns red, shorten text, drop a line, or remove a gradient.
7
Copy the code with the big button under the preview, then paste it in 3dxchat as a gift message. Done.
💡 Tip — click any line in the live preview to jump straight to its input field.
