How It Works
Make a 3dxchat gift in 4 quick steps:
1
Click a template on the right — categories like Sweet, Flirty, Spicy. Your text fills in instantly.
2
Pick a Layout on the left — Center, Inline, Compact, Framed, Minimal, Pyramid, or Custom. Decides how the lines are stacked.
3
Customize (optional) — edit the text fields, change colors via the colored square next to each field, switch fonts with Aa / αв / ꜱᴄ.
4
Copy the code under the preview, paste it in 3dxchat as a gift message. Done!
Good to know
  • Click any line in the preview to jump to that field for editing.
  • Rearrange or remove lines: click the teal ↕ Click to edit pill above the preview — ↑ ↓ buttons appear to swap each line up/down, plus a red × to remove. Click ✓ Done when finished.
  • Hit the ★ next to a template to save it as a favorite (shows up at the top of the templates list).
  • Share copies a link to your clipboard — whoever opens it sees your exact gift in their generator.
  • The counter at the top stays green if your gift fits 3dxchat's limit (240 chars / 255 bytes). Yellow = warning, red = too long.
